I have long been fascinated by topos theory. Initially, and I can still clearly remember this, I watched some videos by Richard Southwell on YouTube. It started out with videos about synthetic differential geometry, which I wanted to use since I was quite fascinated by tangent spaces and geodesics. I assume most game developers are, once they have successfully transitioned from 2d game dev to 3d, and in my case even beyond! My progression was that I started with 2d games like Jump ’n runner, then went to 3d games like WebGLCS and finally went to 4d games like dimensioncrusher.

Richard Southwell started this video series about synthetic differential geometry back then:

In it, he mentioned that this would rely on topos theory, which he has also done videos about:

This also prompted me to ask Prof. Dr. Schröer at the end of Algebraic Geometry I if we could do some topos theory, which he however declined, as he deemed this topic too complex to teach in an algebraic geometry lecture course. This sparked my interest even further, and naturally, after I learned that Prof. Dr. Arndt at HHU published some work on interpretations of dependent product (\(\Pi\)) and dependent sum (\(\Sigma\)) types using model categories, which was something that I needed for my masters thesis, I was hooked, since he is also especially knowledgeable in topos theory.

Dependent typing in category theory

My biggest grief in category theory so far was that it lacked the proper notation for “dependent typing”. Consider the Fuglede-Kadison determinant, which I used in my masters thesis:

In it, I needed to write \[ \det^{\mathrm{FK}}_{\mathcal{R}(G)} : \mathrm{Hom}(V, W) \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0} \] for \(V, W\) some finitely generated \(\mathcal{R}(G)\)-modules. Instead, we would rather write this as a dependent function \[ \det^{\mathrm{FK}}_{\mathcal{R}(G)} : \prod_{V, W \,:\, {}_{\mathcal{R}(G)}\mathtt{HilbMod}^{\mathrm{fg}}} \big(\mathrm{Hom}(V, W) \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}\big), \] so that we do not explicitly need to fix \(V\) and \(W\). I still have not solved this problem, but I am still working on it (I heard for example that categories with families might be one possible approach to that?).

The topos of trees

Well, this led me to research the topic of the topos of trees, as that seemed like an especially easy topic to get started with topos theory and to apply its internal intuitionistic logic.
