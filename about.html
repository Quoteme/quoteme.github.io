About

LinkedIn Github Instagram Reddit Consulting

I am Luca Leon Happel — mathematician, computer scientist, and security researcher based in Düsseldorf.

For over a decade I have worked at the intersection of mathematics and computer science, auditing software deployed in the most heavily regulated environments worldwide: medical devices, clinical systems, and life-critical infrastructure. I have detected and remediated critical security flaws that others missed — not through intuition alone, but through the mathematical rigour that formal methods and deep-tier analysis demand.

I am completing my M.Sc. in Mathematics (HHU Düsseldorf, April 2026), after which I will begin my PhD in Computer Science. I am a Research Associate at the monti project — a medical AI initiative at Düsseldorf University Hospital — where I develop and secure software operating under strict regulatory requirements.

For confidential security consultations, reach me at consulting@happel.ai.
